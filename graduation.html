Graduation

Clearance, fees and guidance for KCU graduands.

6th Graduation Ceremony · 3rd July 2026

Celebrating excellence

Graduation is the moment KCU celebrates the achievements of its graduands. Use this guide to prepare your clearance, fees and ceremony day.

Clearance stages

Confirm that every responsible office has cleared you for graduation, and submit the completed clearance form to the Academic Registrar’s office with proof of payment where required.

  1. Academic Department — signature, stamp, date and remarks
  2. Library — signature, stamp, date and remarks
  3. Finance Office — signature, stamp, date and remarks
  4. ICT Office — signature, stamp, date and remarks
  5. Dean of Students — signature, stamp, date and remarks
  6. Academic Registrar — signature, stamp, date and remarks

Before graduation day

  • Confirm that all tuition and functional fees are fully paid.
  • Complete academic, library, finance, ICT and student affairs clearance.
  • Submit the completed clearance form to the Academic Registrar’s office.
  • Verify spelling and order of names for the graduation booklet and certificate.
  • Pay graduation-related fees and keep proof of payment.
  • Pick up graduation regalia during the official collection window.

Ceremony conduct

  • Arrive early enough for registration, robing and procession formation.
  • Only cleared graduands will be included in the final graduation list.
  • Gowns must be returned in good condition within the communicated deadline.
  • Guests should follow campus security and seating guidance.

Graduation fee

USD 300

Covers the gown, graduation booklet, venue arrangements and academic procession support.

Name or programme corrections?

Contact the Academic Registrar’s office before the final graduation list is published.
