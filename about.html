Who we are

A chartered, innovation-driven university.

King Ceasor University is dedicated to academic excellence, innovation and transformative education. Since its establishment in 2011, the University has grown into a vibrant centre of learning that attracts students from across Africa and beyond, fostering a diverse and multicultural academic community.

With modern learning facilities, experienced faculty, strong industry partnerships and a student-centred approach, KCU nurtures intellectual growth, creativity and professional development. Guided by our motto, “Ignite the Future”, we empower learners to become innovators, problem-solvers and leaders who contribute meaningfully to society.

Vice Chancellor’s message

Welcome to King Ceasor University.

A Chartered institution committed to excellence in education, research, innovation, and service to society.

At KCU, we believe education is transformative, empowering learners with the knowledge, skills, values, and leadership qualities needed to thrive in a rapidly changing world. We provide a supportive and intellectually stimulating environment that encourages critical thinking, ethical conduct, innovation, and academic excellence.

The award of our University Charter reflects our commitment to quality higher education and continuous institutional growth. We are also proud that our MBChB programme has achieved recognition through ECFMG pathways, expanding international opportunities for our graduates.

As we look ahead, we are embracing Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET), advancing research and innovation, strengthening global partnerships, and leveraging technology to enhance learning.

I invite you to join KCU as we transform lives, advance knowledge, and prepare globally competitive professionals and leaders for the future.

Dr. Charity Basaza Mulenga
Vice Chancellor, King Ceasor University
